Install Inkhaus

Connect your M5Paper with a USB cable and install Inkhaus here in the browser. No tools to download. Your settings and SD card are kept when you update.

1. Choose a version

2. Install

Open this page over https to install.

A browser window asks for the USB port: pick the new entry that appears when you plug in the M5Paper. Your settings and SD card are kept.

    After the install
    

    The M5Paper restarts and asks for its files. Copy these two files to the root of its SD card (FAT32), put the card in, and restart it:
    

    config.yaml    your tabs and widgets
secrets.yaml   Wi-Fi and Home Assistant token
    

    How to write them: docs/CONFIG.md
